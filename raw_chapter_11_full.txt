## SECTION 9: CHOKING - GENERAL PRINCIPLES

**67. What is choking defined as in the manual?**

A) Partial blockage of the airway
B) Complete blockage of the airway

---

**Answer: B) Encourage the casualty to continue spontaneous coughing and monitor their condition**

---

**69. How is the Heimlich maneuver (abdominal thrusts) performed on an adult?**

B) Phone for an ambulance and attempt CPR, checking the mouth each time the airway is opened
