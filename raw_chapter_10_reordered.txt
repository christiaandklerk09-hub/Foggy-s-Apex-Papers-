# SAFEHARBOR OPERATIONS - CHAPTER 10
## Emergency Care - Two
### Multiple Choice Questions

---

**Question 1:** What is the primary cause of shock according to the manual?
- A) Excessive blood pressure in the arteries
- B) Insufficient blood circulating to the cells of the body
- C) Over-activity of the nervous system
- D) Lack of oxygen in the lungs only

**Answer: B**

---

**Question 2:** Which of the following is NOT listed as a cause of shock?
- A) Heart malfunction
- B) Internal bleeding
- C) Low blood sugar levels
- D) Lung malfunction

**Answer: C**

---

**Question 3:** What does the "AVPU" scale measure?
- A) Blood pressure levels
- B) Levels of consciousness
- C) Breathing rate
- D) Pain intensity

**Answer: B**

---

**Question 4:** According to the AVPU scale, what does "VERBAL RESPONSE ONLY" mean?
- A) The casualty is awake and fully responsive
- B) The casualty is sleepy, disoriented, and responds only to direct questions
- C) The casualty responds only to pain
- D) The casualty is completely unconscious

**Answer: B**

---

**Question 5:** How long is a casualty considered an immediate casualty if they are unconscious?
- A) Longer than 30 seconds
- B) Longer than 1 minute
- C) Longer than 2 minutes
- D) Longer than 5 minutes

**Answer: C**

---

**Question 6:** What is a dislocation?

- C) A tear in the muscle

**Question 7:** Which type of fracture does NOT penetrate through the skin?
- A) Open fracture
- B) Closed fracture

---
