## MODULE 1: SHOCK

**Question 1:** What is shock in the context of emergency care?
- A) A psychological response to fear
- B) A condition where not enough blood circulates to the cells of the body
- C) A sudden loss of consciousness only
- D) A rapid heart rate unrelated to blood circulation

**Answer: B**

---

**Question 2:** What does shock commonly accompany?
- A) Only minor injuries
- B) No injuries at all
- C) Most injuries to a greater or lesser extent
- D) Only head injuries

**Answer: C**

---

**Question 3:** What can shock lead to if not treated?
- A) Improved consciousness
- B) Increased oxygen supply
- C) Quick death
- D) Natural recovery without intervention

**Answer: C**

---

**Question 4:** What is one cause of shock related to the heart?
- A) Low blood pressure only
- B) Malfunction of the heart or cardiac arrest
- C) Rapid heartbeat with no consequences
- D) Normal heart function

**Answer: B**

---

**Question 5:** How does internal bleeding contribute to shock?
- A) It increases blood supply
- B) It reduces the amount of blood that can be pumped around the body
- C) It has no effect on circulation
- D) It strengthens the heart

**Answer: B**

---

**Question 6:** How does external bleeding contribute to shock?
- A) It improves oxygen delivery
- B) It reduces the amount of blood available to be pumped around the body
- C) It has no impact on circulation
- D) It increases cellular oxygen

**Answer: B**

---

**Question 7:** What lung malfunction can cause shock?
- A) Normal breathing
- B) A breathing problem that reduces oxygen in the blood
- C) Increased oxygen production
- D) Improved lung function

**Answer: B**

---

**Question 8:** How does pain contribute to the development of shock?
- A) It has no role
- B) It always prevents shock
- C) Wounds and dislocations that cause pain can lead to shock
- D) Pain reverses shock

**Answer: C**

---

**Question 9:** What is the primary consequence of insufficient oxygen reaching body tissues?
- A) Improved muscle function
- B) Increased consciousness
- C) Cell damage and potential death
- D) Better circulation

**Answer: C**

---

**Question 10:** Which of the following is NOT mentioned as a cause of shock in the manual?
- A) Heart malfunction
- B) Internal bleeding
- C) Excessive food consumption
- D) Lung malfunction

**Answer: C**
